#Long-term Progression

Map Strategy
Atlas Progression Guide

Master Atlas progression and map strategies to efficiently unlock endgame content

Overview

The Atlas of Worlds is the endgame progression system in Path of Exile. Understanding how to efficiently progress through the Atlas, manage Watchstones, and optimize your map farming is crucial for endgame success.

Atlas Progression Planning

Early Atlas Strategy

When starting Atlas progression:

  1. Complete All White Maps: Unlock all Tier 1-5 maps first
  2. Upgrade Map Tiers: Use Cartographer's Chisels and Orbs to upgrade maps
  3. Focus on Completion: Complete bonus objectives for Atlas completion bonus
  4. Boss Encounters: Defeat map bosses to unlock adjacent maps

Mid Atlas Strategy

  • Tier 6-10: Continue expanding completion
  • Watchstone Acquisition: Start collecting Watchstones from regions
  • Sustained Mapping: Build a map pool for higher tiers
  • Influence Hunting: Encounter Conquerors for Watchstones

Map Selection and Modification

Choosing the Right Maps

Different maps have different layouts and monster density:

  • Linear Maps: Fast clears, good for XP farming
  • Open Maps: High density, excellent for loot
  • Boss Difficulty: Consider boss mechanics when selecting
  • Layout Preference: Choose maps that match your build's playstyle

Map Modification Strategy

  • Alchemy Orbs: Rare maps provide better loot
  • Chisels: Use on Tier 14+ maps for quality bonus
  • Sextants: Apply sextants for additional content
  • Scarabs: Add specific league content (Breach, Delirium, etc.)

Watchstones and Awakening Level

Understanding Watchstones

Watchstones modify Atlas regions and increase map tier levels:

  • Tier Increase: Watchstones raise map tiers in their region
  • Awakening Level: More Watchstones = Higher Awakening Level
  • Modification: Watchstones can have mods that enhance maps
  • Strategic Placement: Place Watchstones to optimize your farming

Awakening Level Benefits

Awakening Level Rewards

  • +2% increased map quantity per Awakening Level
  • Higher tier map drops
  • Better league content spawn rates
  • Access to Awakener's Orbs

Efficient Map Farming Routes

Farming Strategies

  • Strand-Type Maps: Linear layouts for fast clears
  • Dense Maps: High monster count for XP and loot
  • Boss Farming: Target specific map bosses for unique drops
  • Sustained Runs: Build map pools to avoid running out

Special Map Mechanics

Unique Maps

Special maps offer unique rewards and challenges:

  • Vaults of Atziri: Guaranteed unique items
  • Hall of Grandmasters: PvP encounters with unique rewards
  • Unique Boss Maps: Challenging encounters with high rewards

Endgame Content Unlock Path

Progression Order

  1. Complete all white and yellow maps
  2. Acquire Watchstones from all regions
  3. Reach Awakening Level 4
  4. Unlock Sirus encounter
  5. Progress Genesis Tree for long-term bonuses
  6. Unlock Pinnacle bosses
  7. Access highest tier content

Conclusion

Efficient Atlas progression requires planning, strategic map selection, and understanding of Watchstones and Awakening mechanics. Follow this guide to unlock endgame content efficiently and maximize your rewards!
