Graft Items
Deep Dive Guide

Master the Graft items system and unlock powerful character enhancements in PoE 3.27

Overview

The Graft system is one of PoE 3.27's most innovative features. Graft items are special equipment pieces that can be implanted onto your character, providing permanent or semi-permanent bonuses and new active abilities. This system integrates deeply with the Genesis Tree and creates a unique long-term progression path.

What Are Graft Items?

Graft items are special equipment that functions like implants - once equipped, they become part of your character. Unlike regular items, Grafts provide both passive stat bonuses and active abilities that can fundamentally change how you play.

Graft Types

Grafts come in several categories:

  • Limb Grafts: Replace or enhance your character's limbs (arms, legs) - provide active skills
  • Organ Grafts: Internal modifications (heart, brain) - provide passive bonuses
  • Skin Grafts: External enhancements - provide defensive bonuses
  • Specialized Grafts: Unique grafts that interact with specific mechanics like Breach

How to Cultivate Graft Items

Obtaining Grafts

Graft items are obtained through several methods:

  • Genesis Tree Rewards: Unlock Graft nodes on the Genesis Tree
  • Breach Rewards: Complete Breach encounters for Graft drops
  • Boss Drops: Pinnacle bosses can drop high-tier Grafts
  • Graftblood Synthesis: Combine Graftblood at the Genesis Tree to create Grafts

Cultivation Process

Once you have a Graft item, you need to cultivate it:

  • Equip the Graft in your Graft slot (separate from regular equipment)
  • Use Graftblood to upgrade the Graft's power level
  • Complete specific objectives to unlock the Graft's full potential
  • Higher-tier Grafts require more investment but provide significantly more power

Passive and Active Effects

Passive Effects

Most Grafts provide passive bonuses:

Example: Breach Arm Graft

  • +50% increased Breach monster damage
  • 10% chance to gain Graftblood on Breach kill
  • Breach rewards have 20% increased rarity

Active Effects

Some Grafts grant active skills:

Example: Graft Heart Active

  • Active Skill: Graftblood Surge
  • Consumes Graftblood to restore 50% of maximum life
  • Cooldown: 30 seconds
  • Provides emergency healing in tough encounters

Optimal Graft Combinations

Synergistic Combinations

Some Grafts work exceptionally well together:

Breach Specialist Build

  • Breach Arm: Collect Graftblood from Breaches
  • Graft Heart: Use Graftblood for healing
  • Genesis Mind: Increased Graftblood efficiency
  • Result: Self-sustaining Breach farming build

Long-Term Cultivation Strategies

Early League Approach

  • Focus on generic Grafts first (item quantity, survivability)
  • Collect Graftblood from all available sources
  • Prioritize Grafts that enhance your current build

Late League Optimization

  • Transition to specialized, build-defining Grafts
  • Maximize Graft power levels for maximum effectiveness
  • Optimize Graft combinations for your specific build
  • Farm high-tier Grafts from Pinnacle bosses

Synergy with Genesis Tree

Graft items and the Genesis Tree work hand-in-hand:

  • Genesis Tree Nodes: Many nodes enhance Graft effects
  • Graftblood Collection: Grafts help you collect Graftblood for Tree progression
  • Synergistic Bonuses: Tree nodes and Graft effects stack multiplicatively

Advanced Tips

Graft Swapping Strategies

While Grafts provide permanent bonuses, you can swap them:

  • Use different Graft sets for different content types
  • Keep specialized Grafts for specific encounters (bosses, mapping)
  • Some Grafts have cooldowns after swapping - plan accordingly

Conclusion

The Graft items system in PoE 3.27 "Keepers of the Flame" represents a revolutionary approach to character progression. By carefully selecting, cultivating, and combining Grafts, you can create powerful character enhancements that persist throughout the entire league. Invest time in understanding this system - it's one of the most rewarding long-term progression mechanics in Path of Exile history!
