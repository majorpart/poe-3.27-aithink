#Build & Strategy

New Unique Items
Complete Guide

Discover all new unique items and divination cards introduced in PoE 3.27 "Keepers of the Flame"

Overview

Path of Exile 3.27 "Keepers of the Flame" introduces over 20 new unique items, 5 new divination cards, and new base types for wands and rings. These items open up exciting new build possibilities and provide powerful tools for both new and experienced players.

Core New Unique Items

Weapon Uniques

PoE 3.27 introduces several powerful unique weapons, each designed to enable specific playstyles:

🔥 Flamekeeper's Torch

A unique wand that significantly enhances fire spell damage and provides special interactions with Breach mechanics. Perfect for fire-based spellcasters.

  • +150% increased fire spell damage
  • Grants 10% increased Breach monster damage per Breach on map
  • Excellent for Elementalist and Inquisitor builds

⚡ Stormcaller's Edge

A unique sword with lightning damage focus and powerful shock mechanics. Ideal for melee characters who want to leverage lightning damage.

  • Adds massive lightning damage to attacks
  • Shocked enemies take 50% increased damage
  • Great for Duelist and Ranger builds

Armor Uniques

New unique armors provide powerful defensive and offensive bonuses:

🛡️ Genesis Plate

A unique body armor that enhances Graft system interactions and provides significant defensive bonuses. Perfect for long-term progression builds.

  • +30% increased Graftblood gain
  • 50% increased Energy Shield
  • Synergizes with Genesis Tree progression

New Divination Cards

Five new divination cards provide access to powerful items and currency:

  • The Keepers: Set of 8 - Rewards a random unique item from Breachlord drops
  • The Graft: Set of 9 - Rewards a high-tier Graft item
  • The Flame: Set of 7 - Rewards an Exalted Orb
  • The Bloodline: Set of 8 - Rewards a random Bloodline Ascendancy unlock item
  • The Modernization: Set of 6 - Rewards a 6-link item

New Base Types

Wand Base Types

PoE 3.27 introduces new wand base types with higher base damage and interesting implicit modifiers:

  • Flamekeeper Wand: Higher base spell damage, implicit fire damage bonus
  • Breach Wand: Increased cast speed, special Breach interactions

Ring Base Types

New ring bases offer unique implicit bonuses:

  • Bloodline Ring: Increased Ascendancy effect, perfect for dual-class builds
  • Graft Ring: Enhanced Graft effects, synergizes with Graft system

Build Applications

Fire Spellcaster Build

Core Items: Flamekeeper's Torch, Genesis Plate, Bloodline Ring
Strategy: Combine new fire-focused uniques with Elementalist Ascendancy for massive damage. Use Graft system for additional scaling.
Recommended Class: Elementalist or Inquisitor

Dual-Class Build

Core Items: Bloodline Ring, Genesis Plate
Strategy: Maximize dual-class synergies with items that enhance Ascendancy effects. Perfect for advanced players. Learn more in our Bloodline Ascendancies guide.
Recommended Combination: Trickster + Elementalist

Acquisition Methods

  • Breachlord Drops: Many new uniques drop from Breachlord encounters
  • Pinnacle Bosses: High-tier uniques drop from new Pinnacle Atlas Memory Bosses
  • Divination Cards: Farm cards for guaranteed unique rewards
  • Trade: Use asynchronous trading to acquire items from other players

Market Value Predictions

Based on build popularity and power level, here are expected market values:

  • High Value: Flamekeeper's Torch, Bloodline Ring - Expected 5-10 Exalted Orbs
  • Medium Value: Stormcaller's Edge, Genesis Plate - Expected 1-3 Exalted Orbs
  • Budget Options: Most other uniques - Expected 10-50 Chaos Orbs

Conclusion

The new unique items and divination cards in PoE 3.27 "Keepers of the Flame" offer exciting build possibilities and powerful tools for character progression. Whether you're looking for build-enabling uniques or powerful new base types, there's something for every playstyle. Keep an eye on the market and plan your acquisitions strategically!
