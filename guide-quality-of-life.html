Quality of Life
Features Complete Guide

Discover all Quality of Life improvements that make PoE 3.27 more convenient and efficient

Overview

PoE 3.27 "Keepers of the Flame" introduces several significant Quality of Life (QoL) improvements that streamline gameplay and reduce tedious tasks. These features save time and make the game more enjoyable for both new and veteran players.

Identify All Feature

What Is Identify All?

The Identify All feature allows you to identify multiple items at once, eliminating the need to individually use Scrolls of Wisdom on each item.

How to Use Identify All

  • Open your inventory
  • Hold Shift and right-click on a Scroll of Wisdom
  • Select "Identify All" from the context menu
  • All unidentified items in your inventory will be identified

Benefits

  • Time Saving: Reduces identification time by 80%+
  • Inventory Management: Faster sorting and organization
  • Less Tedium: Eliminates repetitive clicking
  • Stamina Preservation: Reduces hand strain during long sessions

Passive Reset System

Overview

The new Passive Reset System makes it easier and more affordable to respec your passive skill tree.

Key Improvements

  • Reduced Cost: Respec points are more readily available
  • Partial Respecs: Reset specific branches without full tree reset
  • Undo Feature: Experimental builds can be easily reverted
  • Free Respecs: Quest rewards include more respec points

How to Use

  1. Open your passive skill tree
  2. Right-click on any passive node to remove it (requires respec point)
  3. Use "Reset Branch" for entire tree sections
  4. Use "Full Reset" only when necessary (costs more respec points)

Asynchronous Trading Convenience

What's New

The asynchronous trading system with Merchant Tabs eliminates the need for both players to be online simultaneously:

  • Merchant Tabs: Set up automated shop tabs
  • Instant Purchases: Buy items immediately when sellers are offline
  • No Waiting: No more coordinating online times
  • 24/7 Trading: Trade anytime, day or night

Other QoL Improvements

Inventory Improvements

  • Stash Tab Improvements: Better organization and search functionality
  • Quick Stack: Automatically organize similar items
  • Currency Tab: Enhanced currency management

UI/UX Enhancements

  • Better Tooltips: More informative item descriptions
  • Improved Filters: Enhanced loot filter customization
  • Performance Indicators: FPS and latency displays
  • Notification System: Better trade and event notifications

Efficiency Improvement Tips

Time-Saving Strategies

  • Batch Identification: Use Identify All regularly to keep inventory clean
  • Plan Respecs: Use partial respecs to test builds without full commitment
  • Merchant Tabs: Set up automated trading for passive income
  • Hotkeys: Learn all new QoL feature shortcuts

Conclusion

The Quality of Life improvements in PoE 3.27 significantly enhance the gameplay experience by reducing tedious tasks and streamlining common activities. Take advantage of these features to maximize your efficiency and enjoyment!
